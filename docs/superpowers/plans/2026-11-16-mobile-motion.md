# Compact mobile panel motion

User request: short, smooth opening/closing and resizing between Tone, Color and Style, with the preview moving continuously; full-screen screens keep their current behavior. Increase the close-button's top and bottom breathing room to match its right inset.

## Final design

- The app animates compact section changes with ordinary Web Animations (FLIP), 180ms natural deceleration and a 150ms exit. The bitmap and border move independently; labels and summaries translate without stretching their text.
- Before changing presentation, capture the currently visible rectangles, including any interrupted motion. After Svelte updates the DOM, animate to the final layout. History/settings remain authoritative and focus follows the presented DOM.
- The live compact panel keeps its control layout fixed during motion; translation and clipping reveal its changing visible height. A temporary layout slot preserves the final flex geometry and the visible bottom edge stays anchored. Closing uses a noninteractive, aria-hidden, ID-free visual copy, retaining its scroll position. Cleanup restores styles and removes temporary nodes on finish, interruption, resize and unmount.
- The preview wrap permits overflow only during the transform, so the larger starting picture is not clipped by the smaller final wrap. Both idle and compact canvas boxes preserve bitmap ratio; CSS size queries and presentation-only resultAspect retain enlargement of small images.
- No animation on Slider updates, ordinary page load, or palette-only changes. Full-screen Photo/style/import use the separate entrance described below. Reduced motion applies immediate state changes; compact motion also falls back when Element.animate is unavailable. No animation library or custom gesture/history interception.
- Close-button spacing is 10px above/below the heading content (11px above including the dividing border), matching the 10px right inset. The body has no redundant top inset. A 48dvh/390px cap retains three complete Tone Sliders and a useful image on 320×640.

Keep native modal focus/keyboard semantics and immediate dismissal. Reduced motion omits spatial movement. Back from style details returns to Style; another Back returns to the preview. Back from the preview follows browser history normally, without inserting an exit trap.

No internal selectors, component copies, dependency edits, live height animation or additional navigation state.
